How the dashboard calculates each metric

Every figure is computed from the supplied College-town home sales dataset. Missing values remain missing and are never replaced with zero.

01

Price

Median sale price includes records with a sale price of at least $1,000. Four lower-price records are excluded from this metric.

02

Sales

Each unique listing_id counts as one completed sale. Repeated street addresses are retained because they may represent separate units.

03

Days on market

Days on market is the time from list_date to contract_date. Only records containing both dates are included.

04

Distance

Campus distance is measured in straight-line miles to the nearest assigned university landmark. The dashboard reports the median.

Data context

Sale records span March 2023 through March 2026, so 2023 and 2026 are partial years. Property types are not used because the source classifications are unreliable.

College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026).
